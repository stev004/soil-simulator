# Phase 9.5 phosphorus structural-loop ablation

## Scope

This analysis isolates the simulator pathway:

phosphorus addition → lower P stress → additional NPP → additional residue
carbon → respiration and modelled SOC change.

It uses five archived non-representative synthetic fields. It is a structural
diagnostic, not a field-effect estimate. No parameter was fitted to improve a
policy ranking.

Carbon quantities are t C ha-1 and yield is t dry matter ha-1. The 10-year
phosphorus schedule has 10 events, GBP 300 ha-1 provisional cost, 50 kg CO2e
ha-1 operational proxy and 150 kg CO2e ha-1 partial lifecycle proxy.

Under the remediated recurring schedules, FYM ranks above phosphorus for the
simple 10-year total-SOC-plus-protected-proxy diagnostic. Excluding phosphorus
therefore leaves FYM first in this five-field analysis. This does not validate
either intervention.

## Conclusion

The current phosphorus response produces a large, horizon-growing structural
effect. The bounded-dose and alternative half-saturation runs show that the
magnitude is assumption-sensitive. With no defensible calibration dataset,
phosphorus magnitudes remain unresolved and phosphorus is excluded from every
headline carbon-management conclusion.
