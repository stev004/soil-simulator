# Claim-boundary register

Numerical claims must also cite their indexed machine-readable source in
`RESULTS_EVIDENCE.md`.

## Permitted claims

- The software is deterministic under frozen configurations.
- Carbon and phosphorus mass balance are enforced.
- The RothC decay core reproduces the documented parity case.
- 56 paired empirical soil–climate locations were used as an empirical pilot.
- Hoosfield source correction improved aggregate uncalibrated fit.
- Effect-prediction and OOD gates failed.
- Downstream selection was correctly blocked.
- Implementation defects identified by independent audit were remediated where
  the indexed closure evidence states so.
- The framework is suitable for methodological and software evaluation within
  the stated boundaries.

## Claims requiring qualification

- Sensitivity rankings are diagnostic because convergence was not achieved.
- Hoosfield performance is mixed because FYM cessation remains weak.
- Empirical-population relevance is limited geographically and numerically.
- `protected_soc` is a model proxy, not measured MAOC.
- Uncertainty coverage is predictive residual coverage within the synthetic
  evaluation framework, not mechanistic or real-field uncertainty.
- FYM calibration is boundary-constrained because the fitted factor reached
  its 1.10 upper bound and the objective was still improving there.

## Prohibited claims

- Validated prediction of real-farm outcomes.
- Causal effects of interventions or field-level recommendations.
- National representativeness.
- Measured MAOC prediction.
- Validated carbon-credit estimates.
- Proof that any intervention sequesters a particular amount of carbon in real
  soils.
- Generalisable ML intervention selection.
- Publication claims based on final-stock R².
- Claims that the sensitivity analysis fully converged.
