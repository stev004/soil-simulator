# Start here: bounded Prism manuscript evidence

Open [`manuscript/PRISM_HANDOFF.md`](manuscript/PRISM_HANDOFF.md) first. The
permitted output is a bounded methodological/software manuscript about the
development, verification and independent auditing of a reproducible
soil–plant–AMF simulation framework, including fail-closed evaluation of
machine-learning intervention-effect prediction.

The central contribution is the transparent engineering and verification of a
modular simulator and evidence pipeline: explicit carbon/phosphorus
accounting, deterministic artifacts, an empirical soil–climate pilot,
long-term SOC evaluation, corrected sensitivity workflow, effect-only ML,
computed readiness gates and the justified decision not to deploy a selector.
