# Permitted manuscript scope

> Development, verification and independent auditing of a reproducible
> soil–plant–AMF simulation framework, including fail-closed evaluation of
> machine-learning intervention-effect prediction.

## Primary recommended paper

A methodological/software paper may cover simulator architecture; the RothC
implementation; crop, phosphorus, AMF and management extensions; mass-balance
enforcement; the empirical soil–climate pilot; long-term Hoosfield evaluation;
global sensitivity workflow; effect-only ML benchmarking; computed readiness
gates; independent audits; and the justified decision not to deploy a selector.

This is the recommended framing. The Phase 9.7 focused independent re-audit
explicitly judges a bounded methodological paper defensible.

## Secondary software paper

A narrower software/simulator article may emphasise reproducibility, modular
architecture, typed contracts, mechanistic accounting, deterministic replay,
testing, artifact manifests and scientific auditability. It must retain all
scientific limitations and may not imply field validation.

## Portfolio case study

A non-journal narrative may cover research direction, AI-assisted
implementation, verification, independent auditing, negative-result handling
and lessons for scientific ML. It must distinguish project/process learning
from biological or agronomic evidence.

## Excluded framing

An agricultural prediction or intervention-recommendation paper is not
supported. The archive cannot support real-farm outcomes, causal management
effects, field recommendations, nationally representative inference, measured
MAOC prediction, carbon-credit estimates or generalisable ML selection.
